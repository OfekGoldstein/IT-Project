**Scope limitation:** All three late-season circuits (Las Vegas, Lusail, Yas Marina) are located in desert climates—spanning cool nocturnal Mojave Desert conditions in Las Vegas (~7–15 °C) and warm Arabian Gulf conditions in Lusail and Yas Marina (~20–30 °C) - rather than temperate European or humid tropical circuits. While conditions are predominantly arid, the dataset captures an isolated desert rainfall event during Las Vegas FP1, providing a localized precipitation test case alongside mostly dry sessions.
